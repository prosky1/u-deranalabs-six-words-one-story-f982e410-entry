Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410): one complete story told in exactly six words.

## The story

> The letter arrived after her funeral.

Six words, 37 characters. A letter that arrives too late: the sender's
words outlive the person they were meant for, and the reader is left
holding a message its addressee will never read. The turn lands on the
last word — everything before it reads as an ordinary delivery, and the
final word rewrites the whole sentence into a story about grief, timing,
and everything left unsaid.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the story verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` (37 characters,
exactly six words), after checking it against the competition gate: 1–60
characters, not only whitespace, exactly six words, one sentence ending
with a period, and no line breaks.

Run it with Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
